History of Vestige

Vestige art museum was founded by Peter S. Moore in May 23, 2021. This place was establish to spread art everywhere. 5 years later, another platform was developed by Vestige, it was Vestige website where artists can share their art digitally. This website was made with no money involved but pure skill and hardwork. The early release of this website was April 15, 2026 and until now this website was still in a test as we continue to improve its functions, code, and security measures.
