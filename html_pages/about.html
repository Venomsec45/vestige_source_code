History of Vestige

Vestige art museum was founded by Peter S. Moore on May 23, 2021. It was established to spread art everywhere. Five years later, Vestige developed another platform: the Vestige website, where artists can share their art digitally. This website was made with no money involved, only pure skill and hard work. It was first released on April 15, 2026, and it is still being tested as we continue to improve its functions, code, and security measures.
