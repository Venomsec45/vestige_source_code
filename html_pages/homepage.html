What is Vestige?

Vestige is a website developed by Shaun where users can express their emotions, inspiration, and love by posting art content digitally. This website was built to make showing art easy and flexible.

Why Vestige?

Vestige is the place where artists can show their art without needing to establish their own art museum. This platform is friendly and accessible to all artists regardless of their look, budget, gender, and skills.

Features of Vestige

  • User friendly
  • Faster help response
  • Strict security measures
  • Responsive buttons
  • Interactive interface
