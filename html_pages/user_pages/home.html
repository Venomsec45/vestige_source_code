About Vestige ARTGO

ARTGO brings together painters, sculptors, photographers and digital artists for a weekend of exhibitions, live work and conversation. As a registered artist, you get a space to show your work and meet collectors, curators and other artists.

Exhibit

Show up to three pieces in the main gallery hall. Each artist gets a labelled space and a short profile in the event guide.

Create live

Join the open studio sessions and work in front of visitors. Materials for sketching and painting are provided on site.

Connect

Meet other artists at the evening mixer, take part in portfolio reviews, and talk with gallery owners looking for new talent.

Event schedule

  1. Day 1

    Arrival and setup

    Check in at the registration desk with your number, collect your artist badge, and set up your exhibit space.

  2. Day 2

    Opening exhibition

    Doors open to the public. Open studio sessions run through the afternoon, followed by the artist mixer in the evening.

  3. Day 3

    Reviews and closing

    Portfolio reviews in the morning, the community showcase in the afternoon, and the closing ceremony at sunset.

Before you travel

  • Save your registration number. You will need it to check in.
  • Bring a valid photo ID that matches the name you registered with.
  • Pack your artwork securely and label each piece with your name.
  • Prepare a short artist bio (two or three sentences) for the event guide.
  • Plan your travel and accommodation early. California venues book up fast.

Questions about your registration? Contact our team and we will get back to you.
